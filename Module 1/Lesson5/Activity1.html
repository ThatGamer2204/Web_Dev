KidsCare is a simple health awareness website designed for children. It provides useful information about staying healthy through exercise, healthy eating, drinking enough water, and getting proper sleep.

1. Why Your Body Needs Exercise 💪

Exercise keeps our bodies strong, active, and healthy. Children should participate in at least 60 minutes of physical activity every day.

Activities such as dancing, swimming, playing football, cycling, and yoga help improve fitness, sleep, mood, and concentration.

2. Stay Healthy – Active Kids 🏃

Playing outdoor games and participating in sports help children stay physically and mentally fit. Regular exercise makes children stronger, happier, and more energetic.

3. Quick Health Tips ☀️

  • 🍎 Eat five portions of fruits and vegetables every day.
  • 💧 Drink enough water to stay hydrated.
  • 😴 Get 9–11 hours of sleep every night.
  • 🏃 Exercise or play for at least 60 minutes daily.
